Question 01 • Single Correct +2.00 / -0.66

Which of the following bodies is constitutional in nature according to the Constitution of India?

💡 View Step-by-Step Solution ↓
🔬 Correct Choice: C. Finance Commission (Article 280)

The Finance Commission of India is a constitutional body established under Article 280 of the Constitution. The NITI Aayog is an executive body created by a Cabinet resolution, and the Central Vigilance Commission was originally created by an executive resolution, later made statutory.

Question 02 • Single Correct +2.00 / -0.66

With reference to ancient Indian history, the term 'Milindapanha' is written in the form of a dialogue in which language?

💡 View Step-by-Step Solution ↓
🔬 Correct Choice: B. Pali

The Milindapanha ('Questions of Milinda') is a Buddhist text dating from around 100 BCE. It records a dialogue in the Pali language between the Indo-Greek King Menander I (Milinda) and the Buddhist sage Nagasena.
