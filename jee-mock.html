Question 01 • Calculus Section +4.00 / -1.00

If a function f(x) satisfies f'(x) = f(x) for all x, and f(0) = 1, then what is the value of the integral from 0 to 1 of f(x) dx?

💡 View Step-by-Step Solution ↓
🔬 Correct Choice: A. e - 1

Given f'(x) = f(x) and f(0) = 1, we solve the simple differential equation to get f(x) = e^x. The definite integral of f(x) = e^x from 0 to 1 is given by: [e^x] evaluated from 0 to 1, which equals e^1 - e^0 = e - 1.
