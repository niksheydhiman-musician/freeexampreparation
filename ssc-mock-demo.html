SSC CGL Tier I — Quantitative Aptitude

Mock Test — Set 01

5 Questions · Each carries 2 marks · 0.5 negative marking · Click any option to see the solution.

1 Quantitative Aptitude — Percentages 2 marks

A shopkeeper marks his goods 25% above the cost price and then gives a discount of 10% on the marked price. What is his overall profit percentage?

💡 View Step-by-Step Solution
1

Understand the problem: Cost Price (CP) = ₹100 (assume). Marked Price (MP) = 25% above CP = ₹125.

2

Apply the discount: Discount = 10% on MP = 10% of 125 = ₹12.50. So Selling Price (SP) = 125 − 12.50 = ₹112.50.

3

Calculate profit: Profit = SP − CP = 112.50 − 100 = ₹12.50.

4

Profit %: (Profit / CP) × 100 = (12.50 / 100) × 100 = 12.5%

⚡ SSC Shortcut Formula

When mark-up = m% and discount = d%,
Net Profit % = m − d − (m×d)/100 = 25 − 10 − (25×10)/100 = 15 − 2.5 = 12.5%

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 12.5%
2 Quantitative Aptitude — Time & Work 2 marks

A can complete a piece of work in 12 days and B can complete the same work in 18 days. They work together for 4 days and then A leaves. How many more days will B need to finish the remaining work?

💡 View Step-by-Step Solution
1

Find individual rates: A's rate = 1/12 of the work per day. B's rate = 1/18 per day.

2

Combined rate: Together = 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36 per day.

3

Work done together in 4 days: 4 × (5/36) = 20/36 = 5/9 of the total work.

4

Remaining work: 1 − 5/9 = 4/9 of the work is left for B.

5

Time for B to finish: (4/9) ÷ (1/18) = (4/9) × 18 = 72/9 = 8 days.

✅ Correct Answer: (C) 8 days
3 General Intelligence & Reasoning — Number Series 2 marks

Find the missing number in the series:
3, 7, 15, 31, 63, ?

💡 View Step-by-Step Solution
1

Look at differences: 7−3=4, 15−7=8, 31−15=16, 63−31=32. The differences are: 4, 8, 16, 32 — each doubling.

2

Identify the pattern: Next difference = 32 × 2 = 64.

3

Find the next term: 63 + 64 = 127.

💡 Alternative view

Each term = (2 × previous) + 1 → 2×3+1=7, 2×7+1=15, 2×15+1=31, 2×31+1=63, 2×63+1=127

✅ Correct Answer: (B) 127
4 English Language — Spotting the Error 2 marks

Identify the part of the sentence that has a grammatical error. If there is no error, choose (D).

(A) Neither of the boys / (B) have submitted / (C) their assignments on time / (D) No error
💡 View Step-by-Step Solution
1

The rule: "Neither of" is followed by a singular verb. "Neither of the boys" is a singular subject.

2

The error: "have submitted" uses the plural form. It should be "has submitted".

3

Corrected sentence: "Neither of the boys has submitted their assignments on time."

📌 Grammar Rule to Remember

Neither / Either + of + plural noun → always takes a singular verb
Similarly: "Everyone", "Nobody", "Each", "Anyone" → always singular verb.

✅ Correct Answer: (B) — "have submitted" should be "has submitted"
5 General Awareness — Indian Economy 2 marks

Which of the following institutions is responsible for formulating and executing India's monetary policy?

💡 View Step-by-Step Solution
1

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) is India's central bank, established in 1935 under the RBI Act. It is the sole authority responsible for monetary policy.

2

Why not the others? SEBI regulates securities markets. The Ministry of Finance handles fiscal policy (taxes, expenditure). NITI Aayog is a policy think tank.

📌 Key GA Facts for SSC (remember these!):

  • Monetary Policy → RBI (repo rate, reverse repo, CRR, SLR)
  • Fiscal Policy → Ministry of Finance (budget, taxation)
  • Securities market → SEBI (Securities & Exchange Board of India)
  • Insurance regulation → IRDAI
  • Competition regulation → CCI (Competition Commission of India)
✅ Correct Answer: (C) Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
